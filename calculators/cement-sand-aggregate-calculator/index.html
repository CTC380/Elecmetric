Cement, Sand & Aggregate Calculator — Nominal Mix Material Estimator

Mode 05 · Advanced material estimate

Estimate cement bags, sand and coarse aggregate for a concrete volume using a nominal mix ratio and a dry-volume factor, with optional water and material cost.

This is a material-quantity estimate, not a structural mix design. Choosing a ratio here does not make the concrete M20, M25 or M30, and does not show compliance with IS 456 or any code. Grade, cement content, water–cement ratio and admixtures must come from the approved mix design and material specification for your project.
Traditional site ratios shown as examples only.
Wet → dry volume. Values around 1.52–1.57 are common in practice; use the factor from your method statement.

Formula

V_dry = V_wet × dry factor × (1 + material wastage) Cement = V_dry × c/(c+s+a) → × bulk density → ÷ bag mass → ⌈bags⌉ Sand = V_dry × s/(c+s+a) Aggregate = V_dry × a/(c+s+a) Water = w/c × cement mass (only if you enter w/c)

    Assumptions and limits

    • Bulk densities are material-dependent and change with moisture. Sand bulking, aggregate grading and moisture are not modelled; test your materials.
    • Quantities are by mix proportion by volume. Weigh-batched or design-mix concrete is specified by mass per m³ and needs its own mix design.
    • Water, admixtures, curing and testing are outside this estimate.
