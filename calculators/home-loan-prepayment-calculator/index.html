How it works

Each instalment is calculated as in Mode 01. A prepayment is applied to the principal right after the instalment you choose, so later interest is charged on a smaller balance. Reduce tenure keeps the EMI and ends the loan sooner. Reduce EMI keeps the original end date and re-computes the EMI on the lower balance after each prepayment. Reducing tenure usually saves more total interest; reducing EMI frees monthly cash flow.

Assumes monthly instalments, a constant rate and prepayments that go entirely to principal. Prepayment charges are paid in cash and are not added to the loan. Estimates only: lenders may have minimum prepayment amounts, timing rules or different recalculation practices. Not financial advice.
