Electrical engineering calculators

Engineering calculators for motors, transformers, cables, short circuit, earthing, generators, solar PV, batteries and airfield lighting. Each page states what the calculator does not cover, and whether independent reference values back it.

These calculators are built for electrical engineers, technicians, estimators and students. Every page shows the formula, a worked example with the steps written out, a table of typical values and the assumptions behind the result, so you can check the working against your standard or design basis. Results come from one tested engine, and you can open any calculator with your own values and print a calculation sheet.

Start with a category below, or pick a calculator by task. For simple unit changes such as kW to HP or psi to kPa, use the Conversion Centre. For quick everyday formulas such as amps to kW or Ohm's law, see the electrical conversions.
