Formula

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert kJ to Watts?

E = P × t (1 W × 1 s = 1 J, 1 kW × 1 h = 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ). Worked example: Energy 36 kJ, time 60 s gives 600 W.

What do I need to enter?

Enter power, energy and time. Leave blank the value you want to find, and the form solves for it. Change the unit of a value with its selector and the result stays the same.

How are watts, kW, joules and kJ related?

One watt is one joule per second, so energy in joules is power in watts multiplied by time in seconds. One kW is 1,000 W and one kJ is 1,000 J. Joules measure energy and watts measure power, so a time is always needed.

How accurate are the results?
