How to Calculate Home Loan Balance Transfer

Cost of a loan = total remaining interest + fees. Each loan is run month by month on a reducing balance (as in Mode 01), starting from the outstanding principal. Net saving = interest on the existing loan − interest on the new loan − all transfer costs. Break-even is the first month in which the interest saved so far has covered every fee. If you finance the fees, they are added to the new principal, which raises the new EMI and interest, but they are still counted once.

The keep current EMI column shows the usual best move when you switch to a lower rate: continue paying the same EMI, so the loan finishes sooner and saves more interest.

Assumes a constant rate on both loans, no change in the property or borrower profile, and no insurance bundling or rate-reset spreads. A floating-rate loan can reset after transfer, so the actual saving can differ (see Mode 05). Some lenders also offer a rate cut to existing customers at a small switching fee, so ask your current lender before transferring. Estimates only, not a lender quotation.
