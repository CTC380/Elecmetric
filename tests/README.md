To add a calculator to the independent set, add its textbook formula to `gen_vectors.py` **without reading engine.js**, regenerate, and commit the JSON. A mismatch means one side is wrong; resolve it against a published worked example.

Coverage: every one of the 157 calculators now has independent vectors. The two calculators added in v1.64.0 (permutations-combinations 32 vectors, binomial-distribution 34) are written from exact integers (`math.factorial`, `math.perm`, `math.comb` and `n ** r` for the counts) and exact `fractions.Fraction` sums of comb(n, j) p^j (1 − p)^(n − j) for each tail (the engine uses whole-number BigInt loops and sums of logarithms), appended from their own random stream so the earlier 2,960 are unchanged; values below 1e-250 are left out of the comparison and expected zeros are left out because the comparison is relative. The two calculators added in v1.62.0 (slope-calculator 34 vectors, distance-calculator 34) are written from exact `fractions.Fraction` differences and 50-digit `mpmath` (the angle by `atan` of the exact slope or of Δz over the horizontal distance, where the engine uses `atan2`), appended from their own random stream so the earlier 2,842 are unchanged; expected values that are exactly zero are left out because the comparison is relative, and an intercept that is only a cancellation residue is not compared. The two calculators added in v1.61.0 (area-calculator 48 vectors, volume-surface-area 36) are written with 50-digit `mpmath` arithmetic from the textbook formulas, appended from their own random stream so the earlier 2,758 are unchanged: the ellipse perimeter is 4 a E(m) with mpmath's complete elliptic integral of the second kind and m = 1 − b²/a² (the engine uses the arithmetic-geometric mean), the regular-polygon area is n s² / (4 tan(π/n)), the sector perimeter is 2r plus r θ, the ring is π (R² − r²) with perimeter 2π (R + r), the cone total surface is π r² + π r × slant, and the pipe total is its two curved surfaces plus two ring ends. The three calculators added in v1.60.0 (pythagorean-theorem 38 vectors, triangle-calculator 35, circle-calculator 32) are written with 60-digit `decimal` arithmetic and exact `fractions.Fraction`: right-triangle angles by arcsine and arccosine of the side ratios (the engine uses atan2), triangle area from 16A² = (a+b+c)(−a+b+c)(a−b+c)(a+b−c) and angles from the half-angle tangent tan(A/2) = √((s−b)(s−c) / (s(s−a))) (the engine uses Heron in a sorted form and the law of cosines), and the circle from π to 60 digits. The calculator added in v1.59.0 (linear-regression, 34 vectors) is written with exact `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic using the normal equations, m = (nΣxy − ΣxΣy) / (nΣx² − (Σx)²), where the engine uses sums about the means; r is the square root of the exact r² with the sign of the slope. The two calculators added in v1.58.0 (matrix-calculator 36 vectors, vector-calculator 32) are written with exact `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic: the matrix determinant by cofactor expansion and the inverse as the adjugate over the determinant (the engine uses Gauss-Jordan elimination with row swaps), and the vector dot and cross products exactly with the angle by arccos (the engine uses atan2 of |A × B| and A · B). The two calculators added in v1.57.0 (simultaneous-equations 43 vectors, linear-interpolation 29) are written with Python `fractions.Fraction`: the equations are solved by exact Gauss-Jordan elimination (a different method from the engine's pivoted floating-point elimination, which it checks against the determinant), with right-hand sides built from chosen whole-number solutions for 20 two-unknown and 16 three-unknown systems plus seven fixed ones (a circuit, a zero on the diagonal, decimal coefficients), and the lines are computed in exact fractions for 22 random cases inside and outside the two points plus seven fixed ones; both are appended from their own random stream so the earlier 2,479 are unchanged. The Fraction Calculator added in v1.55.0 (fraction-calculator) has 39 vectors written with Python `fractions.Fraction` (exact rational arithmetic, automatic reduction, 30 random cases over all four operations with negative numerators and denominators, plus nine fixed cases), appended from their own random stream so the earlier 2,440 are unchanged.
