Number System Converter: Binary, Decimal, Octal and Hexadecimal

Convert whole numbers and fractions between binary, decimal, octal and hexadecimal, exactly and at any size. Fractions that never end are cut off at a digit count you choose, marked, and given as an exact repeating block. For negative numbers, choose a clear representation: a minus sign, or fixed-width two’s complement, one’s complement or sign-magnitude. Every conversion is written out step by step. Calculate.     The methods at a glance

    ConversionMethodExample
    Binary, octal or hex to decimalAdd digit × place value (powers of 2, 8 or 16)1010 = 8 + 2 = 10
    Decimal to binary, octal or hexDivide repeatedly by 2, 8 or 16; read the remainders upward10 ÷ 2: remainders 0, 1, 0, 1 give 1010
    Binary to octal or hexGroup the bits in 3 or 4 from the radix point1010 1010 = AA
    Octal or hex to binaryWrite each digit as 3 or 4 bitsF = 1111
    Decimal fraction to binary, octal or hexMultiply the fraction by 2, 8 or 16 repeatedly; the whole parts are the digits0.625 × 2 = 1.25 → 1, 0.25 × 2 = 0.5 → 0, 0.5 × 2 = 1.0 → 1: 0.101
    Binary fraction to decimalAdd digit × 2^-k for each place after the point0.101 = 0.5 + 0.125 = 0.625

    Questions

    How do I convert binary to decimal?

    Give each bit its place value, a power of 2 counted from the right (1, 2, 4, 8, …), and add the values of the bits that are 1. 10101010 = 128 + 32 + 8 + 2 = 170. The page writes the digits, weights and values in a table, and does the same with powers of 8 and 16 for octal and hexadecimal.

    How do I convert decimal to binary?

    Divide the number by 2 again and again, writing down each remainder, until the quotient is 0. Read the remainders from the last to the first. 170 gives 10101010. For octal divide by 8, for hexadecimal by 16 (remainders 10 to 15 are the digits A to F). The working table shows every division.

    How are binary, octal and hexadecimal related?

    Octal digits stand for 3 bits and hexadecimal digits for 4, so these three convert by grouping, with no arithmetic. Group the bits from the radix point: 1010 1010 is AA in hexadecimal and 010 101 010 is 252 in octal. Converting octal to hexadecimal goes through the bits.

    Why does 0.1 never end in binary?

    A fraction ends in a base only if its denominator, in lowest terms, has no prime factors other than those of the base. 1/10 has a factor 5, which 2 does not have, so 0.1 is 0.0001100110011… with the block 0011 repeating. This page cuts such results off at the digit count you choose (8 to 128), truncated and never rounded, marks them with …, and shows the exact repeating form, 0.0(0011).

    What does 11111111 mean?

    It depends on the representation, which is why you choose one. As an unsigned 8-bit number it is 255. As 8-bit two’s complement it is −1. As 8-bit one’s complement it is −0 and as sign-magnitude it is −127. In fixed-width mode the page shows all four readings of the same bits and marks the one you selected.

    How are negative numbers converted?

    In the default mode the minus sign is kept in front of the digits (−5 is −101 in binary). To get a bit pattern for a negative number, choose two’s complement, one’s complement or sign-magnitude and a width of 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits, and enter a signed decimal value. A value outside the range, such as 128 in 8-bit two’s complement, is refused and the range is given; it is never wrapped around.

    How large can the numbers be?

    Up to 4096 digits, with up to 512 digits after the radix point. The arithmetic is exact (arbitrary precision), so a long number never overflows or loses digits. Fixed-width modes take whole numbers only.

    Can I type a prefix or group the digits?

    Yes. 0b, 0o and 0x prefixes (for binary, octal and hexadecimal), spaces and underscores are accepted, and hexadecimal letters can be upper or lower case. Binary results can be shown in groups of 4 or 8 for reading; the Copy buttons give the plain digits.

    Is anything sent to a server?

    No. The conversion runs in your browser, and the history list is stored only in your browser.
