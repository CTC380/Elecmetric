Frequently Asked Questions

Are Elecmetric calculators free to use?

Yes. The calculators and conversion pages are free to use and run in your browser.

Can I use the calculators directly in my browser?

Yes. The calculators and converters run in your browser and need no installation.

How are calculation formulas determined?

Each calculator uses a standard, published relationship, such as Ohm’s law or the power triangle. The page writes it out with its variables and units defined, so you can check the method before you rely on the result.

Do the calculators show step-by-step working?

Yes. Each result shows its formula, the working step by step and the assumptions behind it.

Can I use the calculators for professional applications?

They are built for engineers, technicians and students, but they use general, standard-neutral formulas and embed no standard tables. Check results against your own code, standard and manufacturer data before relying on them.

How is calculation accuracy verified?

Each calculator runs built-in tests in your browser, and the verification report records the reference checks, standard review and independent review state for every calculator.
