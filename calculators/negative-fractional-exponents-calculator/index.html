    CaseRuleValid when

    How the simplified radical form is found

    Take 725/4. Factor 72 = 2³ × 3². Multiply each exponent by 5/4: 2 gets 15/4 = 3 + 3/4 and 3 gets 5/2 = 2 + 1/2. The whole parts, 2³ × 3² = 72, come out of the root. The fractional parts stay inside the fourth root: 23/4 × 32/4 = ⁴√(2³ × 3²) = ⁴√72. So 725/4 = 72∜(72). The working below is the calculator’s own, for this very example.

    Rules and limits

    • The base and the exponent may be whole numbers, decimals, fractions such as 3/4, numbers with a power of ten such as 1e-3, or negative. The base may also be the constant e, which has no radical form.
    • The exponent is read as an exact fraction and reduced, so 0.75, 3/4 and 6/8 are the same exponent.
    • The simplified radical form needs the prime factors of the base. A base whose numerator or denominator is above 1,000,000,000,000, an exponent with a denominator above 1,000 or a numerator above 1,000, or a form longer than 60 digits is not written out, and the page says so. The value is still given.
    • A negative base with a reduced exponent whose denominator is even has no real value and is refused; complex results are not part of this mode.
    • 0⁰ and 0 to a negative power are refused. Approximate values are double precision, with the number of reliable digits shown.

    Questions

    What are fractional and negative exponents?

    An exponent written as a fraction p/q. The denominator q is a root and the numerator p is a power: a^(p/q) = (q-th root of a)^p. So 8^(2/3) = (cube root of 8)^2 = 2^2 = 4. What is a negative exponent? The reciprocal of the positive power: a^(−n) = 1 / a^n. For a negative fractional exponent both rules apply: 8^(−2/3) = 1 / 8^(2/3) = 1/4 = 0.25.

    How do I type 2/3 as an exponent?

    Type 2/3 in the exponent field. It is read as the exact fraction, not as 0.6666667, so 27^(2/3) is exactly 9. A decimal such as 0.75 is read as the exact fraction 3/4.

    How is the simplified radical form found, and what does “rationalised denominator” mean?

    An exact way to write an irrational power with the root outside the decimal: 12^(1/2) = 2√3, and 2^(−3/2) = √2 / 4. Numbers that can come out of the root do; what stays inside has no factor the root could still remove. How is the radical form found? Factor the base into primes, multiply each prime’s exponent by p/q, take the whole part outside the root and keep the fractional part inside it, then divide the index and the inside exponents by any common factor. For 72^(5/4): 72 = 2³ × 3², giving 72∜(72). What does “rationalised denominator” mean? The root is moved out of the denominator. 2^(−3/2) is 1 / (2√2), which is written √2 / 4 instead. The calculator always gives this form, and the value is the same.

    Why is (−8)^(1/3) allowed but (−8)^(1/2) refused, and does (−8)^(2/6) work?

    The cube root of −8 is the real number −2, since 3 is odd. The square root of −8 is not real, since 2 is even. The calculator checks the denominator of the reduced exponent instead of refusing every negative base with a fractional exponent. Complex results are not given by this calculator. Does (−8)^(2/6) work? Yes. The exponent reduces to 1/3, whose denominator is odd, so the value is −2. Always reduce the exponent first: 2/6 and 1/3 are the same exponent, and only the reduced denominator decides.

    Why does 4^(6/9) give 2∛2?

    6/9 reduces to 2/3, and 4 = 2², so 4^(2/3) = 2^(4/3) = 2 × 2^(1/3) = 2∛2. The calculator shows each step.

    When is the answer exact?

    When every prime in the base has an exponent that the root divides evenly (27^(2/3) = 9), or the exponent is a whole number. Otherwise the radical form is the exact answer and the decimal is a double-precision approximation, labelled as such.

    What is the difference between (q-th root of a)^p and q-th root of (a^p)?

    None in value for a positive base. Taking the root first keeps the numbers small, which is why it is the usual hand method. The calculator lists both forms.

    Is 0 to a fractional power defined?

    0 to a positive fractional power is 0. 0⁰ and 0 to a negative power are refused, because they are indeterminate or mean division by zero.

    Where is the whole-number power calculator?

    Mode 01 (Power Calculator) gives exact BigInt powers and the digit count of huge results. This mode is for exponents that are fractions or negative and shows the root, reciprocal and radical forms.
