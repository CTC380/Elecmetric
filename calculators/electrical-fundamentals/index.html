Electrical Fundamentals Calculators

Ohm's law, AC and DC circuit, single-phase and three-phase calculators with formulas and worked examples, plus related power and energy conversions.

These are the building blocks: the relations between voltage, current, resistance and power, how reactance and impedance behave on AC, and how single-phase and three-phase quantities relate. Use them to check a hand calculation, to find a missing value from two known ones, or to move between line and phase values on a three-phase system. Every calculator shows its formula and working and has unit selectors. For plain unit conversion with no circuit involved (kW to HP, kWh to joules, volts to kV), use the Conversion Centre.

Where to start

  1. Watts, Volts, Amps, Ohms (any two): Find the two unknowns from any two of voltage, current, resistance and power.
  2. Current from Power (kW to Amps): Line current of a three-phase load from kW, voltage and power factor.
  3. Series RLC Impedance: Impedance, phase angle and power factor of a series R-L-C circuit.
  4. Line and Phase Quantities (Star / Delta): Convert between line and phase quantities for star and delta connections.

What these calculators do not cover

These are idealised circuit relations. They assume sinusoidal waveforms and lumped components, and balanced loads unless a calculator says otherwise. They do not model harmonics, cable effects or protection. Use the cable, protection and power systems categories for design checks.
