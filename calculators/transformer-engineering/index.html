How these calculators work together

Choose the rating first, then check how the transformer behaves. Selection takes the load, power factor, growth and target loading to a standard size. The current and loading calculators give the rated current on each side and the spare capacity from measurements. Efficiency and regulation show the losses and the secondary voltage under load.

What these calculators do not cover

Harmonic-rich loads, motor starting, ambient temperature and overload duty can call for a larger or specially rated unit. Standard sizes are the lists shown on each calculator, so confirm availability with your supplier. The transformer fault current calculator gives an upper bound that ignores source and cable impedance.

Cross-check: all 12 calculators listed here are compared with 351 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
