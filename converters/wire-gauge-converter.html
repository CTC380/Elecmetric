SWG and AWG are different systems

Both count sizes with a number, and a higher number is a thinner wire, but the diameters are not the same. Do not swap one for the other without checking the table.

Frequently asked questions

Is SWG the same as AWG?

No. The same number gives a different wire. SWG 18 is 1.219 mm across and AWG 18 is 1.024 mm. Always state which system a drawing or order uses.

How is AWG calculated and SWG found?

AWG follows a formula: d = 0.127 mm × 92^((36 − n) / 39). SWG is a published table of diameters in inches, so it has no formula.

Which is used for cables?

IEC cable sizes are in mm². AWG is used in North American practice and SWG in older British and Commonwealth wire and winding data.
