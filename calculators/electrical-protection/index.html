How these calculators work together

Protection selection depends on three numbers: the current the device must carry, the fault current it must interrupt, and the time it takes to clear a fault. Breaker rating selection picks a standard rating from the design current. The fault calculators give the prospective fault current for three-phase, line-to-line and line-to-earth faults. Cable short-circuit withstand shows whether a conductor survives until the device clears.

What these calculators do not cover

Rating selection is on current only. Breaking capacity, trip characteristic, selectivity and arc flash are separate checks. The Feeder Designer reports breaking capacity as not verified until you enter it, and trip characteristics and selectivity as not checked. Fault currents assume a bolted fault with no arc resistance, and the transformer fault calculator assumes an infinite source, so those values are upper bounds.

Cross-check: all 12 calculators listed here are compared with 417 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
