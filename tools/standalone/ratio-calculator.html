How to simplify a ratio

Divide every term by the greatest common divisor (GCD) of all the terms. The ratio 12:18 has GCD(12, 18) = 6, so dividing both terms by 6 gives 2:3. Ratios with decimals or fractions are first scaled to whole numbers with the lowest common multiple of their denominators, so 1.5:2.5 becomes 3:5 with no rounding. The working for 12:18 is:

  1. Read the two terms in order: 12 : 18.
  2. All terms are whole numbers, so no scaling is needed.
  3. The greatest common divisor is GCD(12, 18) = 6. Euclidean algorithm: 18 = 1 × 12 + 6; 12 = 2 × 6 + 0.
  4. Divide every term by 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2; 18 ÷ 6 = 3.
  5. The simplest form is 2 : 3. Check: GCD(2, 3) = 1.
  6. The order of the terms is kept: 2 : 3 is not the same ratio as 3 : 2.

Use a colon, or the ratio sign ∶, between terms, from two terms up to twelve. A comma is ignored, so 1,000 is read as 1000. Spaces are ignored.

Rules this calculator follows

  • Order is kept. 2:3 and 3:2 are different ratios, so the terms are never sorted.
  • Terms are zero or positive. A ratio compares sizes of like quantities, so a negative term is refused with a message. A term of 0 is allowed and stays 0, so 0:5 simplifies to 0:1.
  • At least one term must be above 0. 0:0 has no simplest form.
  • Exact arithmetic. Terms are held as exact fractions with whole-number (BigInt) arithmetic, with no floating-point rounding in the simplification. Terms can have up to 60 digits.
  • Checked every time. The page rebuilds the entered terms from the simplified ones and cross-multiplies every pair of terms before it says the result is verified.

Ratio, fraction and percentage are different things

RepresentationResultWhat it means
Ratio2 : 3Two parts compared with three parts
Fraction A/B2/3A divided by B (part to part)
Decimal A/B≈ 0.6667The same division as a decimal, rounded to four places
A as a percentage of B≈ 66.67%A compared with B, not with the total
A's share of the total40.0000%2 parts out of 2 + 3 = 5 parts

A ratio of 2:3 means two parts compared with three parts. It does not mean the first part is 66.67% of the total: it is 40% of the total of 5 parts. The first part is 66.67% of the second part, which is a different comparison. The results table and the share table on this page keep the two apart.

Questions

How do I simplify a ratio?

Find the greatest common divisor of the terms and divide every term by it. For 12:18 the GCD is 6, so the simplest form is 2:3. For a ratio with decimals or fractions, scale to whole numbers first. The calculator above does both and shows the working.

How do I simplify a ratio with three or more terms?

Use the GCD of all the terms together. For 6:9:12 the GCD is 3, so the simplest form is 2:3:4. Enter the terms separated by colons, up to twelve of them.

How do I simplify a ratio with decimals?

Multiply every term by the same power of ten to clear the decimals, then divide by the GCD. 1.5:2.5 becomes 15:25, which simplifies to 3:5. The calculator reads each decimal as an exact fraction, so there is no rounding.

Can a ratio have a zero term?

Yes, as long as at least one term is above zero. 0:5 simplifies to 0:1 and 5:0 to 1:0. A division such as A/B is shown as undefined when B is zero.

Why does the order of the terms matter?

A ratio compares the first quantity with the second. 2:3 means the first is two parts for every three parts of the second, while 3:2 means the opposite, so the two are not the same ratio.

What is the difference between a ratio and a percentage?

A ratio compares parts with each other. A percentage of a total compares a part with the whole. In 2:3 the parts are 40% and 60% of the total of 5 parts, while the first part is 66.67% of the second part.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. The history list is stored only in this browser and you can clear it.
