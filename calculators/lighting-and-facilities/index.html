How these calculators work together

These calculators cover building and plant loads: how many luminaires reach a target illuminance, what an LED retrofit saves and how long it takes to pay back, what heater rating raises a volume of liquid by a set temperature, how much energy slowing a fan or pump saves, and how a data centre overhead and cooling load compare with its IT load.

What these calculators do not cover

The lumen method gives an average illuminance estimate, not a point-by-point layout, and does not replace a lighting design to the applicable standard. Savings and payback depend on the tariff, hours and prices you enter. The cooling results are heat load conversions, not a cooling system design.

Cross-check: all 21 calculators listed here are compared with 541 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
