How to Calculate Home Purchase Cost

Loan = (property value − down payment) + costs added to the loan. Initial cash = down payment + costs paid upfront. Total commitment = initial cash + total EMI repayments (principal plus interest). EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula, as in Mode 01.

Percentage costs use the property value (stamp duty, registration and similar) or the base loan before financed costs (processing fee, mortgage charges). Many lenders do not finance stamp duty or registration; check your sanction terms. Interest is charged on financed costs too, which is why the EMI rises. Estimates only, not a lender quotation or legal advice.
