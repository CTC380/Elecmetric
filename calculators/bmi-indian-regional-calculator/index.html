How this was calculated

    Why the classifications differ

    At the same body mass index, South Asian adults tend to carry more body fat, and more of it around the abdomen, than white European adults. For this reason, Asian and Indian references set the overweight cut-off at a BMI of 23 and obesity at 25, instead of 25 and 30. The international reference remains the standard for global comparison.

    The four references used here are not interchangeable diagnostic criteria. The Indian (ESI) and WHO Asia-Pacific tables use similar cut-offs but different category names. The WHO 2004 Asian action points are risk levels for public-health action at 18.5, 23 and 27.5, and are not weight categories. At a BMI of 24.0, the international reference gives Normal and the Indian reference gives Overweight.

    The WHO 2004 expert consultation itself noted that no single BMI cut-off fits all Asian populations. Treat every category as a screening result and discuss it with a doctor.

    Waist measurement

    Abdominal obesity can be significant in Indian adults even when BMI is in the normal range, so guidelines recommend looking at waist circumference as well as BMI. This calculator uses action levels of 90 cm for men and 80 cm for women, from the WHO Asia-Pacific and Asian Indian consensus tables. Measure at the midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone, after a normal breath out. Waist-to-height and waist-to-hip ratios are covered in a separate module.

    Indian & Regional BMI Formula

    BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

    Every input is converted to kg and metres first, so the result does not depend on the unit you enter. Categories are decided on the BMI rounded to 2 decimals, the value shown on screen. The upper limit of each category is exclusive: a BMI of exactly 23.00 is in the overweight category of the Indian reference. The healthy weight range is BMIlower × H² to BMIupper × H² for the selected reference.

    Limitations

    • BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It cannot tell muscle from fat or show where fat is carried.
    • It may mislead for athletes, very muscular people, older adults and during pregnancy.
    • Regional references are not valid for everyone in a region. They are population-level screening tools.
    • For ages under 20, adult categories do not apply. An age- and sex-specific child and teen reference is needed.
    • The healthy weight range is a reference range, not a personal target. Speak to a doctor or dietitian before making health decisions.
