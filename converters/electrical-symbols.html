Electrical Symbols and Quantity Units

Electrical circuit symbols with drawings and meanings, plus a table of electrical quantities, their letter symbols and SI units.

Circuit symbols

SymbolNameWhat it means
Resistor (IEC)Opposes current. The rectangle is the IEC form; the zigzag is the common North American form.
Resistor (zigzag)The same component drawn the ANSI/IEEE way. Check which form a drawing set uses.
CapacitorTwo plates with a gap. Stores energy in an electric field. No polarity.
Polarised capacitorElectrolytic type. The straight plate and the plus sign mark the positive side.
InductorA coil. Stores energy in a magnetic field.
TransformerTwo coils sharing a core. The two vertical lines are the iron core.
DC cell or batteryThe long plate is positive and the short plate is negative. Several pairs in a row mean a battery.
AC sourceA circle with a sine wave: an alternating voltage source.
Earth (ground)A connection to the general mass of earth, used as a reference and for protection.
DiodeConducts from the triangle side (anode) to the bar side (cathode) only.
LEDA diode with arrows pointing away: it gives off light.
Zener diodeA diode with a bent bar. Used to hold a voltage in reverse breakdown.
Switch (SPST)Single pole, single throw. The lever opens or closes the circuit.
FuseA protective element that melts and opens the circuit on overcurrent.
LampA circle with a cross: a lamp or indicator light.
MotorA circle with the letter M. A G in the circle means a generator.
AmmeterMeasures current. Connected in series with the load.
VoltmeterMeasures voltage. Connected in parallel across the part being measured.
NPN transistorA bipolar transistor. Base on the left, collector at the top, emitter (with the arrow) at the bottom.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some symbols have two forms?

Two drawing conventions are in use. The IEC style draws a resistor as a rectangle; the ANSI/IEEE style draws a zigzag. Both mean the same component. Use the one your drawing standard asks for.

How do I tell polarity on a symbol?

On a battery the long plate is positive. On a polarised capacitor the straight plate and a plus sign mark the positive side. On a diode current flows from the triangle to the bar.

Where do I find the full list?

The complete sets are in the drawing standards (for example IEC 60617 and IEEE 315). This page shows the common symbols and does not reproduce those standards.
