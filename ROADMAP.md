## Open work, in priority order
0. **Conversion Centre.** The Level 1 to 3 plan is complete. v1.44.0 closed the last six RapidTables conversion gaps (Ah to Wh, Wh to Ah, Ah to kWh, kWh to Ah, HP to amps, amps to HP). Add direction pages only where the page has something to say beyond the factor.
1. **Go live on elecmetric.com.** The step-by-step checklist, the accessibility test plan and the legal review brief are in `docs/LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`. The domain is decided and the committed tree and workflow already use it. Still to do outside the repo: point DNS at GitHub Pages, set the custom domain and Enforce HTTPS in Settings > Pages, then submit `https://elecmetric.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools (see `DEPLOY.md`).
2. **Real browser and accessibility testing.** A headless Chromium pass (desktop and iPhone 13 emulation, 11 key pages) and an axe-core scan of 135 pages (WCAG A, AA and best-practice) were clean on v1.8.7 and v1.8.8. The axe-core scan was repeated on v1.66.0 over all 520 HTML pages (Chromium 1280 px, light mode, axe-core 4.10.2, WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A and AA plus best-practice): clean after one fix, the 24 px target size of plain list links on 14 Conversion Centre pages. A second pass on nine key pages (phone and desktop widths, light and dark) was also clean, and the colour contrast of the Basic Calculator measures 8.9:1 or better. Axe leaves the contrast of the page header and gradient headings for manual review (it cannot work out the background), so in v1.67.0 that contrast was measured from the rendered pixels instead (Chromium, light and dark, 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 px): the header text of 12 pages and every heading on them was measured against the worst-case background pixel behind it. The calculator, converter, reference and legal pages were all at or above 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text). On the app view (`index.html`) the 10 px orange labels of the header status strip (ENGINE, TESTS, BASIS, THEME, DIGITS) measured 3.4 to 4.3:1 and the green PASS text 4.3:1 on the lighter right of the header gradient; both were lightened (`#FFDDB8` about 4.8:1, `#B4F3CC` about 4.9:1) and re-measured clean. This is a pixel measurement of the gradient, not a substitute for a look on a real screen. The headless Chromium pass on iPhone 13 emulation has not been repeated. Still needed: a real phone and desktop, keyboard and screen-reader checks of the app view, Feeder Designer, the calculator pages and the offline cache.
3. **Confirm standards conventions.** The size lists (breaker, transformer, CCR, IEC 60228), the cable temperature coefficients and the AS/NZS 3000 5% figure come from the sources named in `STANDARDS.md` and `tests/README.md`. Check them against your own edition or manufacturer data.
4. **Review the category hub wording** (`tools/category_hubs.js`). The prose and the cross-listings (for example the cable and insulation calculators under Airport and AGL) are an editor's judgement and need a domain review. The Airport and AGL hub now has seven primary calculators (CCR loading, maximum fittings, isolating transformer, CCR input energy, brightness steps, lamp life curve, light intensity at distance) and is still the thinnest; more AGL calculators would strengthen it. All seven now have a guide, and six of them have a dedicated AGL formula guide (v1.45.0). More AGL guides (lamp, cable and circuit design topics) would build the library further.
5. **Licensed standards clause review and individual professional reviewer records (v1.73.2).** Stage 3 is now scope-screened for all calculators: 50 applicable standards identified, 107 justified as Not applicable. Exact clause/table verification against licensed, project-adopted editions and individual professional sign-off remain open. A user-directed team-level reviewer designation for all 157 calculators is recorded in `tools/team_review_approval.json`. This is not a named individual or independent engineer sign-off. No individual technical review is recorded or claimed: the earlier `approved` records for all 157 calculators (reviewer "Mr. Metric") were removed in v1.73.6 because no named reviewer with signed worksheets is on file. Add an `approved` record to `tools/review_records.json` only when a named, qualified person has actually reviewed the calculator and the evidence is kept. No calculator is Verified yet: Verified still needs an exact formula reference and a clause-reviewed standard where one applies.
6. **Operator details and legal review.** Set at the site owner's direction (v1.73.10): `operator_name` is "Rahul Sharma" and `operator_location` is "India" (`tools/site_info.json`), so the deploy guard passes. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` are drafts written to match what the app does (no accounts, analytics or server calls; no fonts or scripts from other hosts). The contact address is set in `tools/site_info.json`; have a lawyer review both, and run a trademark search for the Elecmetric name before launch.
7. **Mixed continuous and non-continuous loads** in the Feeder Designer: done in v1.8.9 (optional "Continuous part of a mixed load", NEC-style basis only). Several separate loads are entered as one total and one continuous kW.
8. **Breadth** (Phase 3, 250 calculators). Thin divisions: 01 Fundamentals, 22 Control, 33 Building, 35 AGL, 36 Converters, 20 Harmonics, 26 Renewable, 30 Safety. Each new calculator comes with vectors in the same change.
9. **Real HTTP API and embeds**, multilingual support, one-click error report. The client-side API is documented in `docs/API.md` (generated by `node tools/build_api_docs.js`, checked by the tests); the hosted REST API is planned and not built.
10. **Known capability gaps (benchmark against other platforms, 12 November 2026).** Recorded so claims stay within what the package does:
    - **Manufacturer data:** none is embedded. `data-templates/` holds blank CSVs for user-supplied ampacity, grouping, temperature and k-factor tables. A verified cable, transformer, CCR, protection and AGL component database with source and revision tracking would be new work.
    - **AGL photometrics:** one tool, `agl-light-intensity-distance` (Allard's law with fog/MOR), plus the general lighting calculators. There is no isolux or grid calculation and no check of results against ICAO Annex 14 photometric requirements. Annex 14 and Doc 9157 are cited as references only.
    - **LED payback:** `led-retrofit-payback` is a general lighting calculator, not AGL-specific.
    - **Project-level reporting:** the Feeder Designer and cable selection check produce one text report with a pass/fail status and a "not verified" list. There is no report that combines several calculators, and none with revision history or reviewer details.
    - **Public verification page:** the verification register, standards status and review pack exist in `docs/`; a public page presenting each calculator's formula, standard edition, clause, test vectors and review status is not built. Do not describe calculators as Verified or traceable until item 5 is done.
    - **Wording:** avoid "project-ready", "verified" or "traceable" in positioning until the items above are done.

## Rules
- **New calculator:** definition, formula, assumptions, self-tests, and an independent vector written from a textbook formula without reading `engine.js`, merged together.
- **Release:** bump `VERSION` in `engine.js` and `CACHE_VERSION` in `sw.js` together, update "Current status" above, add the change to the history file, regenerate pages with `node tools/build_pages.js`, and run both test commands.
- **History:** version-by-version notes live in `docs/ROADMAP_HISTORY.md`. Do not rely on "still open" lines there.
