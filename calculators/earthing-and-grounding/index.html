How these calculators work together

Earthing design starts from soil resistivity and ends with a measured value on site. The calculators estimate the resistance of a single rod, of several rods allowing for mutual interference, and of a buried grid. The neutral earthing resistor calculator sizes a resistor for a chosen earth-fault current, and the fault loop impedance check confirms that an earth fault draws enough current for the protective device to operate.

What these calculators do not cover

Soil resistivity varies with moisture, temperature and depth, so a calculated resistance is an estimate to be confirmed by measurement on site. Step and touch voltage, transferred potential and lightning protection are not calculated here.
