Add or Subtract Time from a Date

Pick a start date, choose add or subtract, and enter any mix of years, months, weeks and days. You get the exact result date with its weekday, day of the year and ISO week. Months and years follow the calendar, not 30-day or 365-day approximations, and the month-end rule is stated on the page.

How to add or subtract time from a date

For days and weeks the calculation is simple: each date is a whole day number, so adding 30 days is adding 30 to the day number and converting back. Because no clock time is involved, a time zone or daylight-saving change never moves the answer.

Months and years need a rule, because months have 28 to 31 days. This calculator keeps the day of the month where the target month has it, and otherwise uses the target month's last day. It always counts from the original start date, so a chain of months does not drift: 31 January plus 1 month is 28 February (29 in a leap year), and 31 January plus 2 months is 31 March.

When a period has several parts, the months and years go first and the weeks and days second. 1 January 2026 plus 1 year, 2 months, 1 week and 3 days is 1 March 2027 after the months, then 11 March 2027 after the 10 days.

Week number and day of year

The result also shows its day of the year (1 to 365, or 366 in a leap year) and its ISO week. ISO weeks run Monday to Sunday, and week 1 is the week with the year's first Thursday, so 29 to 31 December can belong to week 1 of the next year and 1 to 3 January can belong to week 52 or 53 of the previous year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the date 90 days after a given date?

Enter the start date, choose Add, and put 90 in the Days box (or press +90). For example, 90 days after 18 November 2026 is 16 February 2027.

How do I subtract days from a date?

Choose Subtract and enter the number of days. 30 days before 18 November 2026 is 19 October 2026. Enter the amount as a positive number; the operation decides the direction.

What is 31 January plus one month?

February has no 31st, so the result is the last day of February: 28 February in a common year and 29 February in a leap year (31 January 2024 + 1 month = 29 February 2024).

What happens if I add a year to 29 February?

The result is 28 February of the next year, since 29 February exists only in leap years. 29 February 2024 + 4 years is 29 February 2028.

Can I add years, months, weeks and days together?

Yes. Years and months are added first as calendar months, then weeks and days as calendar days. The Working section shows the date after the months step and the final date.

Is a month 30 days?

No. A calendar month is 28, 29, 30 or 31 days. Adding 1 month to 15 January gives 15 February, which is 31 days later. Use the Days box if you want an exact number of days.

Does it skip weekends or holidays?

No. This mode counts calendar days. Adding working days with a weekend and holiday calendar is the Business Days mode.

How is the ISO week number worked out?

By the ISO 8601 rule: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year. The result shows the ISO week-year too, because it can differ from the calendar year around 1 January.

Why does adding 1 year not always equal adding 365 days?

A year is counted on the calendar. Adding 1 year to 1 January 2026 gives 1 January 2027 (365 days), but adding 1 year to 1 January 2028 spans a leap day and is 366 days. Choose days if you need exactly 365.

What is the supported date range?

1 January 0001 to 31 December 9999. A result outside that range is refused with a message. Before 1582 the Gregorian calendar is extended backwards, so very old dates are a calculation, not a historical record.
