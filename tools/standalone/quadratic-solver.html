History

The quadratic formula

For ax² + bx + c = 0 with a ≠ 0, the roots are x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / (2a). The part under the root, Δ = b² − 4ac, is the discriminant. Its sign decides what kind of roots the equation has. The formula follows from completing the square: divide by a, move c/a across, add (b/2a)² to both sides, and take the square root.

Exact arithmetic, not floating point

Every coefficient is held as an exact fraction of whole numbers, so 0.1 is exactly 1/10 and not a binary approximation. The discriminant, its sign and the roots are calculated exactly. This matters at the edges. For 0.1x² + 0.2x + 0.1 = 0 the discriminant is exactly zero and the page reports one repeated root. There is no tolerance that could call a tiny non-zero Δ zero, or the reverse. Numbers can be very large or very small without overflow, because nothing is held as a floating-point value until the final decimal is written.

Roots that are not whole or fractional are written as radicals in simplest form, such as 1 ± √2, with the decimal beside them. Each root is then substituted back into the equation in exact arithmetic and must give exactly zero.

Questions

Why does it not show an error when the discriminant is negative?

A negative discriminant means the parabola does not cross the x-axis, so there are no real roots. The equation still has two roots in the complex numbers, and the calculator gives them as a ± bi. For x² + 2x + 5 = 0 they are −1 + 2i and −1 − 2i.

Can I enter fractions?

Yes. Type 3/4, a mixed number such as 2 1/2, a decimal such as 0.75 or a number in scientific notation such as 1e-6. Fractions are kept exact.

What happens when A is zero?

Then x² drops out and the equation is linear. The calculator solves it as bx + c = 0 and says so. If A and B are both zero there is either no solution (C is not zero) or every number is a solution (C is zero).

How is an answer checked?

Each root is substituted into A·x² + B·x + C using exact arithmetic, including roots that contain a square root or i, and the result must be exactly zero. The engine is also tested against independent reference values from a computer algebra system.

Is anything sent to a server?

The calculation runs in your browser. The history list is stored only in this browser and you can clear it. This page also loads Google Tag Manager; data collected by tags depends on its configuration.
