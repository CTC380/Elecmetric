How these calculators work together

A cable has to pass several checks and none replaces the others: it must carry the current after derating, keep voltage drop within the limit, and survive a fault until the protective device clears it. The calculators here cover each check separately so you can see which one decides the size. Work from voltage drop or ampacity, confirm short-circuit withstand, then check physical limits such as parallel sets and conduit fill. The insulation resistance tools support testing once the cable is installed.

What these calculators do not cover

Tabulated ampacities are not built in: cable derating needs a base rating from the standard or the manufacturer table, and regional table lookups are not implemented. Sizing for voltage drop does not check ampacity or fault withstand, so confirm all three before selecting a cable.

Cross-check: all 21 calculators listed here are compared with 519 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
