Quartile methods

Quartiles are not defined the same way everywhere, so the same data can give different Q1 and Q3. This calculator shows which convention is in use and lets you switch.

Inclusive (default) position h = (n − 1)p + 1, linear interpolation. Excel QUARTILE.INC, R type 7. Exclusive position h = (n + 1)p, linear interpolation, held to the first/last value when h falls outside 1…n. Excel QUARTILE.EXC, R type 6. Tukey hinges Q1 and Q3 are the medians of the lower and upper halves; the overall median is left out of both halves when n is odd.

For 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9: inclusive 4 and 5.5, exclusive 4 and 6.5, Tukey 4 and 6.

Formulas
Mean x̄ = Σx / n Median middle value of the sorted data (mean of the two middle values when n is even) Mode most frequent value(s); "no mode" when every value occurs once Range max − min IQR = Q3 − Q1 Variance σ² = Σ(x − x̄)² / n s² = Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1) Mean absolute deviation MAD = Σ|x − x̄| / n (about the mean, not the median) Standard error SE = s / √n Coefficient of variation CV = s / |x̄| × 100 % Sum of squares Σx² (raw) SS = Σ(x − x̄)² (corrected, about the mean)

CV is only meaningful for ratio-scale data (a true zero) and a mean away from zero. It is not shown when the mean is exactly zero, and a warning appears when |mean| is smaller than the standard deviation.

Verification

All values except the three square-root results (SD, SE, CV) are exact fractions. Square roots are rounded once, correctly, at the precision chosen. Mean, variance and standard deviation use the same exact engine as Mode 01.

Limits of this calculator

Descriptive statistics summarise the data you enter; they do not say whether the data are correct. Standard deviation and CV measure spread, not accuracy, and are not a substitute for calibration, measurement uncertainty evaluation or the acceptance criteria of the applicable standard. A mean and median that differ, or a large IQR, are prompts to look at the data, not conclusions. Treat as an estimate and have a competent engineer review it before it is relied on.
