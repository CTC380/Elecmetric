Worked example: 18/24

ParameterResult
Original numerator18
Original denominator24
Greatest common divisor6
Simplified numerator3
Simplified denominator4
Simplified fraction3/4

The greatest common divisor of 18 and 24 is 6. Divide both by 6: 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4, so 18/24 = 3/4. The two fractions have the same value, because multiplying or dividing the top and the bottom by the same non-zero number does not change a fraction.

Ways to simplify a fraction

MethodHow it worksExample: 18/24
Greatest common divisorFind the largest number that divides both parts, then divide both by itgcd(18, 24) = 6, so 18/24 = 3/4
Euclidean algorithmDivide, keep the remainder, repeat with the divisor and the remainder until the remainder is 0. The last divisor is the gcd24 = 18 × 1 + 6; 18 = 6 × 3 + 0; gcd = 6
Prime factorsWrite both numbers as products of primes and cancel the common primes18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3; common 2 × 3 = 6
Repeated divisionDivide both by any common factor, such as 2 or 3, until none is left18/24 → 9/12 → 3/4

The calculator uses the Euclidean algorithm for the greatest common divisor, because it is exact for numbers of any size, and shows the prime factors as well when the numbers are small enough to factor quickly.

Questions

What does lowest terms mean?

A fraction is in lowest terms, also called simplest form, when its numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1. Then the greatest common divisor is 1 and the fraction cannot be reduced further.

How do I know if a fraction is already simplified?

Find the greatest common divisor of the numerator and the denominator. If it is 1, the fraction is already in lowest terms. The calculator says so and does not change the fraction. 17/5 is an example.

Is the simplified fraction equal to the original?

Yes. The working ends with a check: the cross products of the two fractions are equal, so they have the same value.

What happens with negative fractions?

The sign is moved to the numerator and the denominator is kept positive. So 7/−14 and −7/14 both give −1/2, and −6/−8 gives 3/4.

What is 0 over a number?

Zero over any non-zero denominator is 0. Every number divides 0, so the greatest common divisor of 0 and 7 is 7, and 0/7 = 0/1 = 0.

Can I simplify a decimal fraction such as 1.5/2.5?

Yes. Both numbers are multiplied by the same power of 10 to make them whole numbers, here 15/25, and then reduced to 3/5.

How large can the numbers be?

Each number can have up to 400 digits. The arithmetic uses whole numbers of unlimited length, so the greatest common divisor and the result are exact at any size. The prime factors are shown only when the numbers are small enough to factor quickly.

Why is the decimal sometimes shown with brackets?

A fraction has a repeating decimal when its denominator, in lowest terms, has a prime factor other than 2 and 5. 1/3 is 0.(3), where the 3 repeats forever. The calculator marks this instead of showing a rounded value as exact.

Is anything sent to a server?

Everything runs in your browser. The history of your last ten simplifications is kept only in this browser and can be cleared with one button. This page also loads Google Tag Manager; data collected by tags depends on its configuration.
