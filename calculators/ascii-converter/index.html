Questions

What is ASCII?

ASCII is a 7-bit character code. It gives 128 characters a number from 0 to 127: control codes (0 to 31 and 127), the space, digits, letters and punctuation.

What is the ASCII code of A?

65 in decimal, 0x41 in hex, 01000001 in binary. Small a is 97, 0x61.

Why is é rejected?

It is not in ASCII, which has no accented letters. It is U+00E9 in Unicode. Use the Unicode converter to see its UTF-8 and UTF-16 bytes.

What about codes 128 to 255?

Those belong to extended 8-bit sets such as ISO 8859-1 and Windows-1252, which differ from one another. This page covers standard ASCII only.

What are control characters?

They are non-printing codes from the teletype era, such as LF (line feed, 10), CR (carriage return, 13), HT (tab, 9) and ESC (27). They are still used in serial protocols and terminals.
