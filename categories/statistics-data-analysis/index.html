Assumptions and Limitations

Statistics describe the data you enter. Small samples, outliers and non-independent readings limit what a result can show, and a calculator cannot tell whether a test is the right one for your data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Statistics & Data Analysis tools does Elecmetric have?

This build has 9 published tools in Statistics & Data Analysis, and 8 more are planned. The planned tools are listed on this page and are not available yet.

Are the upcoming Statistics & Data Analysis tools available?

How are the Statistics & Data Analysis tools checked?

Each published calculator runs automated tests and shows its formula, working and assumptions. The verification status on each tool page records what review has actually been done, and no tool is marked independently verified unless its page says so.
