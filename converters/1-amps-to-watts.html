1 Amps to Watts

1 A is 230 W at 230 V and 120 W at 120 V (single-phase, power factor 1). Table for DC and three-phase, power factor 0.8, and the formula.

Power230 W

1 A at 230 V, single-phase, power factor 1 =
230 W

Formula

P = V × I (DC) P = V × I × pf (single-phase) P = √3 × V × I × pf (three-phase)

Worked example

  1. P = 230 × 1 × 1 = 230 W
  2. With a power factor of 0.9: P = 230 × 1 × 0.9 = 207 W

What 1 A looks like

1 A is a phone charger, a small lamp or a device on a small plug-in adapter.

This is an estimate of the load current only. Choosing a cable or a breaker also needs the installation method, the ambient temperature, the voltage drop and the rules you work to.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 1 amps?

1 A is 230 W on a 230 V single-phase supply and 120 W on 120 V, both with a power factor of 1. The power depends on the voltage, so it is a different answer on every supply.

What if the power factor is not 1?

The real power falls. At 230 V and a power factor of 0.9, 1 A is 207 W. Heaters and filament lamps are close to 1, while motors and many power supplies are lower.

What about a three-phase supply?

1 A on 400 V three-phase at a power factor of 0.8 is 554.3 W. The line voltage is used with the factor √3 (about 1.732).
