How to read it

Each period, interest = opening balance × rate per period; principal = EMI − interest; closing balance = opening − principal. Early EMIs are mostly interest; the principal share grows every month. The last instalment is adjusted so the balance closes at exactly ₹0. The financial-year view groups instalments by the date they fall due (1 April to 31 March), which is how interest certificates are usually organised.

Assumes a constant rate and no prepayment. Your lender's statement may differ slightly because of day-count conventions, rounding and the actual disbursement and EMI dates.
