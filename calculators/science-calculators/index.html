Upcoming Science Calculators

Planned order, based on how often the calculation is needed and how clearly its result can be checked. The order can change.

  1. Kinematics (SUVAT) Calculator

    Planned, number 1 of 8

    Solve motion with constant acceleration from any three known quantities.

    Formulav = u + at; s = ut + ½at²; v² = u² + 2as

    You enter: Any three of u, v, a, s, t.

    You get: The remaining quantities.

  2. Projectile Motion Calculator

    Planned, number 2 of 8

    Range, maximum height and flight time of a launched object.

    FormulaR = v² sin 2θ / g; H = v² sin²θ / 2g; T = 2v sin θ / g

    You enter: Launch speed, launch angle, gravity.

    You get: Range, height and time of flight.

  3. Ideal Gas Law Calculator

    Planned, number 3 of 8

    Solve for pressure, volume, amount or temperature of an ideal gas.

    FormulaPV = nRT, with R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)

    You enter: Any three of P, V, n, T.

    You get: The fourth quantity, with units.

  4. Density, Mass and Volume Calculator

    Planned, number 4 of 8

    Find any one of density, mass or volume from the other two.

    Formulaρ = m / V

    You enter: Two of density, mass, volume.

    You get: The third, in your chosen units.

  5. Work, Energy and Power Calculator

    Planned, number 5 of 8

    Work done, kinetic and potential energy, and power.

    FormulaW = F × d × cos θ; KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh; P = W / t

    You enter: Force, distance, mass, speed, height or time as needed.

    You get: Work, energy in joules, power in watts.

  6. Molarity and Dilution Calculator

    Planned, number 6 of 8

    Concentration of a solution, and the volume needed to dilute it.

    FormulaM = n / V; M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

    You enter: Moles, volume, or the starting and target concentration.

    You get: Molarity, or the volume of stock and solvent.

  7. pH and pOH Calculator

    Planned, number 7 of 8

    pH from hydrogen ion concentration, and back.

    FormulapH = −log₁₀[H⁺]; pH + pOH = 14 at 25 °C

    You enter: Hydrogen ion concentration or pH.

    You get: pH, pOH, [H⁺] and [OH⁻].

  8. Molar Mass Calculator

    Planned, number 8 of 8

    Molar mass of a compound from its formula.

    FormulaM = Σ (atoms of element × atomic mass)

    You enter: A chemical formula such as H2SO4.

    You get: Molar mass in g/mol with the breakdown by element.

How These Calculators Will Work

Every calculator on Elecmetric shows the formula it uses, the working step by step, and a worked example. Inputs have units and sensible limits, and results state what was assumed. A planned calculator is published only when it meets that standard.

Assumptions and Limitations

These will be textbook models. The gas law assumes an ideal gas, projectile motion ignores air resistance, and pH assumes dilute solutions at 25 °C. Real systems at high pressure, high concentration or high speed need more than these formulas. The molar mass tool depends on an atomic mass table that must be checked against IUPAC values before it is published.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these for homework or for professional use?

They are general-purpose calculators with the working shown, so they suit both. For safety-critical or regulated work, check the result against your own standard and data.

Which science calculator will be built first?

The Kinematics (SUVAT) Calculator, then the Ideal Gas Law Calculator. The order on this page is the planned order and can change.

How are planned calculators chosen and published?

Each one is built on a standard formula, shows its working and assumptions, and is published only after it has worked examples and has been checked against separately written reference calculations. Until then it stays on this page as planned.
