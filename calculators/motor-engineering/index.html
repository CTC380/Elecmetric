How these calculators work together

Motor calculations start with full-load current and the starting duty. Full-load current sizes the cable and protection. Starting current and the resulting voltage dip decide whether the supply can start the motor and which starting method is acceptable. Torque and slip describe what the shaft delivers. The starting calculators show how star-delta and auto-transformer starting trade starting current against starting torque.

What these calculators do not cover

Locked-rotor current, efficiency and power factor come from the motor data sheet, and the calculators do not read manufacturer curves. Starting torque is compared with direct-on-line only. The load torque curve and acceleration time are not modelled. Check protection settings against the motor thermal limits separately.

Cross-check: all 16 calculators listed here are compared with 334 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
