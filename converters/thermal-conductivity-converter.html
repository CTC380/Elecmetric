Common conversions

  • 1 W/(m·K) = 0.5777893165 BTU/(h·ft·°F)
  • 1 BTU/(h·ft·°F) = 1.730734666 W/(m·K)
  • 1 kcal/(h·m·°C) = 1.163 W/(m·K)

Heat in cables and enclosures

Cable insulation, soil and enclosure materials are rated by thermal conductivity, or by its inverse, thermal resistivity. Copper is about 401 W/(m·K) and dry soil is below 1.

Temperature intervals

The temperature in these units is an interval, so °C and K are the same size and no offset applies. A Fahrenheit interval is 5/9 of a kelvin, which is built into the BTU factor.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula to convert W/(m·K) to BTU/(h·ft·°F)?

BTU/(h·ft·°F) = W/(m·K) × 0.5777893165. For example, 401 W/(m·K) is 231.6935159 BTU/(h·ft·°F).

How do I convert BTU/(h·ft·°F) back to W/(m·K)?

W/(m·K) = BTU/(h·ft·°F) × 1.730734666. The Swap button above reverses the direction for any pair of units.
