How these calculators work together

Small-signal and instrumentation work needs quick, reliable arithmetic: the series resistor for an LED, the output of a voltage divider, the cutoff of an RC filter, the gain of an op-amp stage, the frequency of a 555 timer. The instrumentation tools scale a 4-20 mA loop to the process value and convert Pt100 or Pt1000 resistance to temperature.

What these calculators do not cover

Component values are treated as ideal, and tolerances are not modelled. Check every design against the device data sheet, including the absolute maximum ratings.

Cross-check: all 25 calculators listed here are compared with 749 reference calculations written separately from the engine. This confirms the arithmetic and selection rules. It does not confirm that a standard table or convention matches your standard or manufacturer data.
