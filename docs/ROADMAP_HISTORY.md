This is the version-by-version log as it stood at v1.5.13 plus the F3 and F5 to F8 rounds, kept unchanged apart from this note. "Still open" lines below were true on the day they were written and are **not** the current status: for that, read `ROADMAP.md`. Where a line says "v1.5.14, not in this tree", that work (F3) is now merged and ships in 1.5.14.

Source: *Deep Web Research: Building a World-Class Electrical Calculator Platform*. Status below is from reading the repo on 2026-11-07. Items marked (verify) were found by keyword only and need a hands-on check.

## Already in place (v1.4.x)

- 113 calculators across 33 of the 36 divisions; unit engine with per-field unit switching

## Gaps against the research doc, in priority order
1. **Verification coverage**: every calculator now has independent vectors (Issue 6). Remaining gaps are partial coverage of generator-kva (standard sizes, rated current) and rack-cooling (airflow), and the standard-size lists, which are conventions to confirm against your own standard or manufacturer data.
2. **Standards bases**: add IS (India), BS, AS/NZS as selectable bases. Method constants only, with licensed tables loaded as user-supplied data. Largest differentiator in the doc (section 7, 20).
3. **Indexable pages** (first cut shipped in v1.5.7: `node tools/build_pages.js` writes 46 intent pages plus a hub into `pages/`; `BASE_URL=https://your.site/elecmetric` adds canonical links and sitemap.xml. Still open: one page per calculator beyond those 46, FAQ blocks, per-page structured data): the app is one `index.html`. The doc's SEO plan (section 8, 16, 17) needs one crawlable URL per calculator with formula, worked example, assumptions, FAQ and related links, generated from the registry at build time so the engine stays the single source.
4. **Breadth to Phase 3 (250)**: still thin divisions are 01 Fundamentals, 22 Control, 33 Building, 35 AGL (1 calculator), 36 Converters (2), 20 Harmonics, 26 Renewable, 30 Safety. Add in layers, each with vectors in the same commit.
5. **Real HTTP API and embeds** (section 24): today `Engine.api` is a JS function. A thin serverless wrapper plus an embeddable widget.
6. **Excel/PDF export** (section 25): CSV and print exist (verify true .xlsx and PDF).
7. **Natural-language "What should I calculate?"** (section 12): a `route()` exists (verify depth). Keep the rule that AI picks the calculator and the engine computes.
8. **Multilingual, reviewer/version trust panel, one-click error report**: not yet.

## v1.5.13 changes (third audit, F2)
- F2 closed: new calculator `ac-ohms-law` (AC Ohm's Law (Phasors), division 02). Enter any two of voltage, current and impedance, each with a phase angle in degrees; the third phasor and the power triangle come back. V = I·Z (magnitudes multiply, angles add), S = V·I* (angle ∠V − ∠I), P = |S| cos θ, Q = |S| sin θ (positive Q is inductive), pf = cos θ. Angles wrap to (−180°, 180°]. A blank angle on an entered value counts as 0°; an angle on the value being solved is rejected; three values or one value are rejected; an impedance angle beyond ±90° warns (negative resistance).
- Search: "ac ohms law", "ohms law ac", "phasor calculator", "ohms law with phase angle" and "complex impedance ohms law" now land on it first; "ohms law calculator" still goes to the any-two solver. Six new router cases (49 in all).
- Links: the DC Ohm's law and any-two solver list it under Next; the Ohm's law and watts-volts-amps-ohms landing pages carry a chip to it and their FAQ answer on AC now points to it. The 46-page set is unchanged.
- Tests: 12 self-tests, 36 new independent vectors (`gen_vectors.py`, Python `cmath`, written without reading engine.js; the earlier 1,158 vectors are unchanged), 6 router cases.
- Still open from the audit: F3 energy cost, F5 remaining phrasings (power-unit converter, "amps to kw 3 phase", mm² voltage drop), F6 unit prefixes, F7 copper resistivity hint, F8 bill labelling.

## v1.8.9 (Feeder Designer: mixed continuous and non-continuous load)
- New optional input **Continuous part of a mixed load** (kW) in the Feeder Designer. On the NEC-style basis the breaker rating and the cable check use the equivalent current Ieq = Ib × ((P − Pc) + 1.25 × Pc) / P, that is non-continuous load plus 125% of the continuous part. Left blank, the existing Continuous / Non-continuous choice applies exactly as before; when entered it overrides that choice. A value above the total load, or negative, is rejected.
